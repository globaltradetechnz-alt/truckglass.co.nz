At Truck Glass, we make the insurance claims process simple and hassle-free. We direct bill all major insurance companies for truck windscreen replacement and repair, so you don't need to worry about paperwork or upfront costs.

On most policies, there is no excess for glass claims. We handle the entire claims process from start to finish, liaising directly with your insurer to ensure fast approval and payment.

What You Need to Do

  1. Call us on 0800 611-731 with your vehicle details
  2. Provide your insurance policy number and company name
  3. We verify your coverage and confirm nil excess on glass with your insurer
  4. We book the replacement or repair at your convenience
  5. We complete the work and bill your insurer directly

Our insurance team has been handling truck glass claims since 2008. We know the process inside out and work with all major insurers to get your vehicle back on the road quickly.
